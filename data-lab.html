Data Classification

OFFICIAL DATA

Directly reproduced from government/intergovernmental sources. Source and year always cited.

DERIVED METRIC

Calculated from official data with a transparent formula. Methodology always stated.

ILLUSTRATIVE

Used only when data is unavailable. Always clearly labeled — never presented as measured statistics.

Key Metric Definitions

FTAForeign Tourist Arrivals — visitors crossing international borders into India
FTEForeign Tourist Expenditure — subset of FTA paying for tourist services
DTVDomestic Tourist Visits — visits by Indian residents (not unique visitors)
ForexForeign Exchange Earnings — reported by Reserve Bank of India
GDP %Total tourism contribution (direct + indirect) as % of India's GDP — WTTC methodology
