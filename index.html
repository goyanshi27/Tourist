Data Classification

OFFICIAL DATA

Directly from government sources. Source and year always cited.

DERIVED METRIC

Calculated from official data with transparent formula. Methodology always stated.

ILLUSTRATIVE

Used only when data is unavailable. Always clearly labeled.

Key Metrics

FTAForeign Tourist Arrivals — visitors crossing international borders
DTVDomestic Tourist Visits — not unique visitors
ForexForeign Exchange Earnings — reported by RBI
GDP %Total tourism (direct+indirect) as % of GDP — WTTC methodology
